# Cancer Care

A blog that explains cancer in plain language and helps people support friends and family going through it.

## About this blog

Cancer affects almost everyone, directly or through someone they love. But the science can be confusing, and it is hard to know what to say or do when someone you care about is diagnosed. This blog aims to:

- **Explain** how cancer works and how it is treated, in clear, jargon-free language
- **Guide** friends and family on how to show up for someone going through it
- **Share** reliable, well-sourced information that people can trust

## Who it's for

- Friends and family of someone with cancer who want to help but are not sure how
- Students and curious readers who want to understand the basics of cancer biology
- Anyone looking for trustworthy, easy-to-read information

## What you'll find here

| Topic | Example posts |
|---|---|
| **Understanding cancer** | What is cancer? How do tumors form? What do "stages" mean? |
| **Treatment basics** | What are chemotherapy, radiation, and immunotherapy? |
| **Supporting someone** | What to say (and not say), practical ways to help, supporting caregivers |
| **Prevention and screening** | Risk factors, screening guidelines, myths vs. facts |
| **Research in plain English** | Summaries of recent studies and what they mean for patients |

## Important note

**This blog is for education only. It is not medical advice.** It cannot diagnose, treat, or replace guidance from a doctor or healthcare team. Anyone with questions about their own health should talk to a qualified medical professional.

## How I handle sources

Every post will:

1. Link to the sources used (for example, the National Cancer Institute, American Cancer Society, or peer-reviewed studies)
2. Include the date it was written or last updated, since medical information changes
3. Explain things clearly and honestly, without exaggerating or giving false hope
